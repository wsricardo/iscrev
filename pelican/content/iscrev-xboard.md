I'm super excited to showcase **iScrev XBoard**, a complete and modern digital whiteboard designed specifically for teachers, content creators, and students!

iScrev XBoard is much more than just a blank canvas. It was built to revolutionize the way you teach and learn, packed with amazing features tailored to boost your productivity and interaction.

![iScrev XBoard Web Interface](/images/iscrev_xboard_ui.png)

### Main Features

- **Integrated Lesson Recording:** Capture and share your lessons or explanations with a single click. No need for third-party screen recording software.
- **Native PDF Support:** Import your slides, worksheets, or PDF materials directly into the whiteboard and annotate them in real-time.
- **Cross-Platform:** Access it from anywhere! Available as a fast and responsive Web version (PWA) and as a robust native Desktop application.

![iScrev XBoard Desktop Version](/images/iscrev_xboard_desktop.png)

### Under the Hood / Tech Stack

The project was entirely built using a powerful modern ecosystem:
- **JavaScript, HTML, and CSS** for the user interface and interactivity.
- **Node.js and Electron.js** delivering the best performance and advanced features in the desktop version.

Our goal is to provide a seamless experience, no matter what device you are using. Try out **iScrev XBoard** and transform your lessons and presentations today!

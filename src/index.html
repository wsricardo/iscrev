About the Project & Creator

The iScrev Ecosystem

iScrev Notes and iScrev XBoard were born from the need to unify text, mathematical formulas, and sketches without breaking the creative momentum.

Instead of fragmented tools, we offer a cohesive experience inspired by classic notebooks, designed for students, teachers, and professionals.

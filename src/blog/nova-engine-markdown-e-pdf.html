New Markdown Engine and Improved PDF Generation

junho 09, 2026 WSRicardo atualizacoes, markdown, pdf, desenvolvimento, arquitetura

Continuing our rapid pace of improvements to iScrev Notes, we've just released updates that completely change how the application understands what you type and how it turns it into an exportable document.

If the last update focused on project support and code cleanup, this one focuses entirely on stability and user experience during writing and drawing.

The New Internal Markdown Engine

Until today, iScrev used a very simple system (based on Regular Expressions) to convert your text into bold, lists and quotes.The problem with this approach is that it was fragile: mixing paragraphs with lists or quotes often broke the formatting of your note.

To honor the "Zero Dependencies" philosophy (we didn't want to inflate the application by installing third-party plugins like marked.js), we built our own "State Machine Parser" from scratch.

The new engine reads the text line by line, intelligently memorizing the logical state of the structure.This gave us instant powers:

  1. Native Tables: Now you can build classic Markdown tables.They have their own CSS wrapper, allowing individual horizontal scrolling if they are too wide.
  2. Multiline Code Blocks: Using the three backticks (```), developers can now store long snippets of code with the correct font and independent scrolling areas.
  3. Indestructible Lists: Now you can break lines at will between the items in your list without breaking the HTML semantic structure.

And all this without compromising in any way the anti-XSS protection or the accuracy of our built-in LaTeX engine.

The "Physics" of Paper and Perfect Export to PDF

The second major focus was to solve the feared problem of Pen desynchronization on screens of different sizes.

In the past, if you wrote something and used the pen to highlight, and then moved the application to a smaller window, the text would "shrink" (it would reflow and break lines prematurely), but the SVG of the pen would stay in the same place.The result?Completely scrambled notes and PDFs.

Now we harden digital paper. When used on desktop, the notebook will always preserve a crystallized aspect ratio of "A4 bond" size (~800px).If you squeeze the window, the paper will not "crumple";Instead, we'll cleverly create a scrollbar for you to navigate it.

With this, we combine two scenarios: - Your art and pen highlights will never be misaligned with the typed text again. - When clicking on "Save PDF" (Ctrl+P / Command+P), the browser will always read the sheet in A4 format, generating PDFs with an absurd cutting fidelity, without depending on heavy PDF converters installed on the machine.

On mobile, we use intelligent behavior: the layout floats freely (100%), allowing for pleasant edge-to-edge reading and typing without forced side scrolling.

We continue to evolve the architecture to ensure iScrev is the lightest, most secure, and fastest hybrid note-taking tool you can access in your browser.

Enjoy the improvements and feel free to use the new tables and code blocks!

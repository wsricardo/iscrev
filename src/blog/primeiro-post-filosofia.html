The Beginning of Everything - The Philosophy Behind iScrev Notes

abril 15, 2026 WSRicardo filosofia, design, webdev

The Journey Begins

Welcome to the iScrev Notes blog!This space was created to share the development journey, design decisions and philosophy that make this project unique.

iScrev Notes was born from a simple premise: digital writing tools don't need to be cold or complex.We believe in welcoming computing, where technology serves as a bridge, not a barrier, to thought.

Our Pillars

  1. Local-First: Your data is yours.Everything you write is stored in your own browser.
  2. Operational Simplicity: No build steps, no heavy frameworks.What you see in the source code is what runs in the browser.
  3. Design with Intention: The color palette, typography and layout were chosen to create a calm and focused environment, ideal for study and reflection.

This is just the beginning.Follow us for more updates, tutorials, and insights into the future of iScrev Notes.

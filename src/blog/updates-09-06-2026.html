June Updates to iScrev Notes: Support via Stripe, New Layout and Clean Code

junho 09, 2026 WSRicardo atualizacoes, stripe, pix, apoio, desenvolvimento, design

We have reached another important stage in the evolution of iScrev Notes.June brought news not only visually, but focused on the long-term sustainability of the project and the internal quality of the code.

As an independent project, iScrev Notes grows with time, research, and community support.The main focus of this update was to make this support more accessible, global and secure, as well as ensuring that the application remains light and fast.

Global Support with Stripe and Simplified PIX

The biggest technical news aimed at the community is the integration with Stripe.Until recently, support for the project was limited to the Brazilian public through transfers via PIX.With the new update, users around the world can now support iScrev Notes quickly and securely using credit cards.

The design of the support page (support.html) and the internal modal in the diary also underwent a redesign.The premise was to embrace minimalism and honesty.We removed complex forms and value checkboxes that added friction.Now the flow is as clean as possible:

  • For Brazil (PT): The user has direct access to our PIX key to copy, paste into your bank and define the value you feel in your heart, in addition to being able to use Stripe.
  • International (EN): The Stripe button takes you directly to the global checkout, with a suggested value starting from 1 dollar, but allowing the contribution to be free.

This approach aligns with our philosophy: no fine print, hidden fees or long processes.Just one click or "copy and paste".

Deep Cleaning and Goodbye to Legacy Code

Developing a local-first app means having an obsession with keeping the code small, fast and functional offline.During this update, we did a real “cleanup” of the codebase (src/):

  1. Goodbye to the Old PDF Exporter: At the beginning of the project, we had a heavy library focused on generating PDFs.Since we moved to a refined native print CSS (window.print()), this asynchronous exporter has become obsolete.Today it was officially deleted, reducing the weight of the package and our Service Worker.
  2. Variable Optimization: The code for configuring obsolete values, currencies and states (which previously governed the old support page) has been completely swept from support.js.
  3. New Cache: We have released version v10.7 of our offline cache, ensuring that browsers pick up the lighter version of the app.

Structured and Synchronized Blog

And, as you might notice if you're reading this from our root directory, we've adjusted the way our static blog interacts with the app!

We've refined Pelican's architecture to ensure that categories, tags, RSS/Atom feeds, and articles don't "leak" into the main site root.Everything now lives harmoniously inside the /blog/ subfolder.This means that iscrev.com keeps your landing pages pure at the root, and textual content and updates are perfectly delimited.

Next Steps

We still have a lot of work to do.The project's next big leap will involve further modularizing the main journal file, embracing ES Modules in full force.This will open the door to building more ambitious features in the future, while preserving the “one file” essence.

Thank you very much to everyone who uses, writes, draws and supports iScrev Notes.We continue forward!

To support the project and help us keep this ad-free and privacy-focused tool, visit our support page.

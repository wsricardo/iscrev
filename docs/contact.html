Contact

Get in touch with the iScrev Notes project

This page provides a public contact channel for general questions, privacy requests, feedback, technical issues and project-related messages.

If you want to report a problem, suggest improvements, ask about privacy or request clarification about how the site works, this is the most appropriate first contact channel.

Because the project still follows a lean philosophy, contact currently happens directly by email. This keeps the structure simple and avoids introducing a form workflow that is not yet part of the site.

  • Main email iscrev.tech@gmail.com
  • Good topics for this channel Early support, privacy questions, feedback, product suggestions and editorial or technical matters.
  • Language You can write in Portuguese or English.

When to use it

Messages that fit this page

Privacy and data

Questions about the privacy notice, third-party resources, local storage behavior and user-rights requests.

Technical issues

Interface bugs, export problems, rendering issues, mobile navigation problems, local saving or app behavior.

Suggestions and collaboration

Feature ideas, UX refinements, editorial proposals and conversations related to the project’s growth.

Helpful contact habits

How to make your message more useful

If your subject is technical, it helps to mention the browser, device, a short description of the issue and, when possible, the steps needed to reproduce the behavior you saw.

If the topic involves privacy, say clearly what you need, such as clarification, correction, deletion or review of a published statement.
